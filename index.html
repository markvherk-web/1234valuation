In English: independent business valuation

1234valuation provides independent, written valuations of companies and ownership interests. It is for owners who are considering a sale, a change in ownership or an investment, and who want a clear, well-founded view of what their business is worth. When you are ready to sell, I can also help as a business broker.

What you get

  • A written conclusion on the value of the business within the agreed basis of value
  • An analysis of the company’s financials and the drivers of its earnings
  • An explanation of the methods used (market-based and DCF) and the key assumptions
  • A sensitivity analysis showing what moves the value
  • The main value drivers and risks
  • A walkthrough of the result with me

Price and process

From DKK 50,000 excl. VAT for a standard assignment. More complex assignments are priced after a short scoping call. Scope, deliverables, timing and fee are agreed in writing before I start. The process has three steps: a free 20-minute call, a written agreement, and then analysis and a walkthrough of the result with you.
